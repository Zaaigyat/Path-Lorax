OUR PURPOSE

Opportunity should be within reach.

Path Lorax helps young people explore possible careers, build job-readiness skills, and discover resources for finding decent work. We support UN Sustainable Development Goal 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth.

🌍 OUR STORY

Growing opportunities, together.

Path Lorax is a student-focused career guidance project designed to make career exploration and job-readiness resources easier to approach.

🌱

Our mission

Help students and job seekers explore interests, develop practical skills, and discover useful resources for finding decent work.

🌤️

Our vision

A future where young people can access clear career guidance and take informed steps toward meaningful work.

🌍

Our SDG 8 goal

Support decent work and inclusive economic growth through career awareness, skill-building, and links to employment resources.
